Origin

From an internal system to operational software.

"Good technology doesn't stand out. It works."— OrionQuant Technologies Team

The foundation of Orion Quant Technologies was established in mid 2023 through internal development work. Over an extended period, the software was developed, tested, and refined without a product label or company structure — used privately with proprietary capital and in simulation environments.

During this phase, the system underwent multiple fundamental revisions. News automation and volatility logic proved to be critical areas requiring repeated redevelopment. The focus remained on functionality, stability, and security rather than marketing.

Orion Quant Technologies was established as a company once this technical foundation demonstrated clear value for organizational use. Today, OQT builds on this proven infrastructure and continues to develop the system.
